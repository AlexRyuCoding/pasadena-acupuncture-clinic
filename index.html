IN 4 ｜ What this is

Old medicine, and it is simpler than you think.

Acupuncture is very old. It began in China more than 2,000 years ago.

Thin needles go into a few spots on your body. They are much thinner than the needles used for shots. Several fit inside one shot needle.

The needles do not add anything to you. They ask your body to start healing. Your body already knows how. If you cut your finger, it closes on its own.

Does it hurt? Almost never. You may feel a small pinch for a second. Most people feel heavy, warm, or very relaxed.

HOLD 7 ｜ Herbs

Herbs keep working after you go home.

Chinese herbs are the other half of this medicine. People have used them for more than 3,000 years.

A formula is made for you. Two people with the same pain can get two different formulas, because they have different bodies. There is no one size that fits all.

Most people take herbs as granules in warm water. They keep working between visits.

Ask whether herbs are right for you at your visit.
